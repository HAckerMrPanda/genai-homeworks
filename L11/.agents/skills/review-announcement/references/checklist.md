# Review checklist

Dates and times: day, month, year, start time and time zone.

Duration and format: how long the session lasts, whether it is online or in person,
and the stated platform or location.

Conditions: price, whether registration is required, how to register and its deadline.

Claims: whether the topic, audience and prerequisites match the factual source.
Do not promise an outcome absent from the source.

Every factual correction must be supported by the supplied source.
