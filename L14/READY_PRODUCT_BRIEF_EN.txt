Build a prototype B2B wholesale supplier portal.
Buyers see their assortment and contract prices, and order by text, voice, file
or catalog. The app prepares a draft with items, quantities and total. Quantities
follow case sizes; availability is checked without exposing numerical inventory.
The buyer selects a delivery address and submits the order.
The manager sees the original input and prepared lines, makes a decision,
forwards the order to fulfilment and updates its status. Buyers see history and
can repeat orders. Start with sample data. Use a model capability for text/audio
interpretation; calculate prices and totals from catalog data with ordinary code.
