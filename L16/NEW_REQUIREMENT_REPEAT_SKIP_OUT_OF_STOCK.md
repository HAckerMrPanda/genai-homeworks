# New requirement (from the L14 product review)

**Problem.** "Repeat order" copies every line of a previous order into the draft, including products that are now **out of stock**. The buyer can submit them again, and the manager has to reject or fix the same line every time.

**Request.** When a buyer repeats an order, do not put out-of-stock products into the new draft. Tell the buyer clearly which products were left out and why. Everything else (available and limited products, quantities in cases, delivery address) should be repeated as before. The original order must stay exactly as it was.
